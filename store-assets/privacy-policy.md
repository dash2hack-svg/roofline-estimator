# Roofline Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Roofline Estimator stores customer names, phone numbers, addresses, roof measurements, product selections, and estimates locally on the device.

## Data use

The app uses this information only to prepare roofing estimates and saved job records. It does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Location

When the user chooses “Use my location,” browser or device location permission is used only to provide a starting point for address/property lookup. Exact location data is not submitted by the app without a separate integration configured by the organization using it.

## Local storage

Saved jobs and product catalogs remain in browser or app local storage until the user clears app data or deletes the saved records.

## Third-party services

Live address, satellite, supplier, payment, and customer communication integrations may exchange job data only when those integrations are explicitly enabled and configured.

## Contact

Questions about data or deletion should be sent to the app owner through the contact address listed in the store listing.

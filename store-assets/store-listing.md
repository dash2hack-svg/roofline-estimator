# Roofline Estimator

## Short description

Measure roofs, calculate materials, and create accurate roofing estimates in the field.

## Full description

Roofline helps roofing contractors estimate projects from one mobile workspace.

Features include:
- Existing-property address lookup and manual/AR-ready measurement modes
- Multiple roof planes with independent area and pitch
- Waste-factor calculations and automatic square conversion
- Exact shingle, underlayment, ice-and-water, and ridge-cap selection
- Editable supplier prices and custom product catalogs
- Material quantity calculations
- Labor, tear-off, disposal, permit, tax, and markup inputs
- Local saved jobs and proposal previews
- Mobile-friendly PWA and native Android/iOS packaging

Measurements and estimates should be verified in the field before materials are ordered.
